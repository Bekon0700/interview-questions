# Behavioral — Questions

Top companies weight behavioral heavily (Amazon's Leadership Principles especially). Answer with STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) and use real examples from your CV.

---

## Beginner / warm-up

1. Tell me about yourself. (2-minute pitch)
2. Why are you looking to leave Rokomari / why are you interested in this role?
3. What are your strengths and weaknesses?
4. Where do you see yourself in 3-5 years?

## Core behavioral

5. Tell me about a time you took ownership of a project from scratch. (your CV: bootstrapped affiliate platform)
6. Tell me about a time you led a project or initiative. (your CV: led Next.js migration)
7. Describe a time you had a conflict or disagreement with a teammate or manager. How did you resolve it?
8. Tell me about a time you had to learn a new technology quickly.
9. Describe a time you missed a deadline or made a mistake. What did you do?
10. Tell me about a time you improved performance or optimized something. (your CV: 37s→1s, 11s→3s)
11. Describe a time you had to collaborate across teams. (your CV: cross-functional work)
12. Tell me about a time you received difficult feedback. How did you respond?
13. Describe a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information.
14. Tell me about a time you handled a production incident under pressure.
15. Describe a time you disagreed with a technical approach. How did you handle it?

## Growth / career

16. You started as a fresher and grew into an engineer. Tell me about that journey.
17. Your CV mentions interest in Golang-based full-stack development. Why, and how are you pursuing it?
18. How do you keep your skills up to date?
19. What kind of team/engineering culture do you thrive in?
20. Do you have any questions for us? (always prepare 2-3)
